A deaf dog keeps barking at an empty armchair — so whatever is sitting in it, he can see it.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the story (exactly two sentences, 206 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The story

> Ever since we moved in, our dog has barked at the empty armchair in the corner of our bedroom every night, and nothing we do makes him stop. The vet just told us he has been completely deaf for over a year.

The second sentence reframes the first: a deaf dog cannot be barking at a sound, so it must be barking at something it sees in the chair. The horror is implied, never explained.
